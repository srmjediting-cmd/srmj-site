About SRMJ

SRMJ is two people: Sebastian Rogat and Magnus Jørgensen. We enhance photographs of rentals and guest houses, and we deal with every client ourselves. We are both from Denmark. Sebastian works from Denmark, and Magnus is in Milan, Italy, for now on an exchange, which is why our postal address is in Milan.

How we work

  • Honest edits only.We correct light, color, sharpness and straight lines. Nothing is added and nothing that belongs to the place is hidden.
  • You see everything first.All edited photographs are sent with a watermark. You decide what to use, and you pay only for that.
  • Your originals stay yours.We never alter the original files, and we delete your details when you ask us to.
