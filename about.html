About SRMJ

SRMJ is two people: Sebastian Rogat and Magnus Jørgensen. We enhance photographs of rentals and guest houses, and we deal with every client ourselves.

How we work

  • Honest edits only.We correct light, colour, sharpness and straight lines. Nothing is added and nothing that belongs to the place is hidden.
  • You see everything first.All edited photographs are sent with a watermark. You decide what to use, and you pay only for that.
  • Your originals stay yours.We never alter the original files, and we delete your details when you ask us to.
