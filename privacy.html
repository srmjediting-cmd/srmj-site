Privacy notice

Last updated 3 November 2026

We keep as little about you as we can, we say where we found it, and we delete it when you ask.

Who we are

SRMJ (Sebastian Rogat and Magnus Jørgensen), Via Luca Ghini, 20141 Milano, Italy. Email: info@srmjbusiness.com. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

If we wrote to you first

We contact owners of rental properties whose contact details are published on their own website or listing page. For that we keep:

  • your name and email address, as published by you;
  • the link to your property or website, and where we found your details.

To make the free sample in our first email, we use one or two photographs you have published. The sample is sent only to you. We do not publish it.

If you work with us

We also keep the photographs you send or approve, our emails with you, and the payment confirmation from PayPal. We use them only to do the work, deliver it and keep the records the law requires.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Fonts and images are served from the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Why we may use your data

  • To offer our service to your business (legitimate interest). You can object at any time.
  • To deliver work you have asked for (contract).
  • To keep accounting records (legal obligation).

Who else sees it

We never sell or share your data for marketing. We use these services to run the business: Google (email and file sharing), PayPal (payments) and GitHub (this website).

How long we keep it

If you reply "no thanks", we stop writing and delete your details within 10 business days. We keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, so that we never write to you again. If you work with us, we keep your data for as long as the work and the accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or to stop using it. Write to info@srmjbusiness.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
