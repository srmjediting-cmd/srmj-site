Questions hosts ask

How did you find me?

Through the contact details you publish on your own website or listing page. Our first email says where we found them. Reply "no thanks" and we will not write again.

Will my place look different from reality?

No. We correct light, colour, sharpness and straight lines. Nothing is added and nothing that belongs to the place is hidden, so guests find what the photographs show. Our standard

When do I pay?

Only after you have seen every edited photograph with a watermark and decided to use them. Payment goes through PayPal. How payment works

What do you need from me?

A link to your listing or website, or the photographs themselves. That is all.

Where can I use the edited photographs?

Anywhere you like: Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com or your own website. They are your photographs.

What happens to my photographs and details?

We use them only for your edits. We never publish them without your permission, and we delete your details when you ask. Privacy notice
